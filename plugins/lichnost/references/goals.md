# Goals — `Личная/Цели.md`

One note with the owner's goals. It is what the reviews measure the period against and what every
piece of advice must serve. Goals appear at any time, from three places:

- **He says so** — «поставь цель …», «хочу к весне …». `idea` records it.
- **An idea grows into one** — when `idea` captures or refines an idea, it asks whether the idea
  serves an existing goal or is itself a goal.
- **A review notices one** — a theme he keeps returning to for weeks, a wish repeated in the diary,
  a problem that keeps costing him. The review proposes the goal in its questions.

A goal is written, changed, reached or dropped only after his «да» — Claude proposes, he decides.
The yearly review goes over all goals at once; it is a revision, not the only moment goals appear.

## Shape of the note

```markdown
---
updated: <YYYY-MM-DD>
tags:
  - цели
---

# 🎯 Цели

[[Личная]]

## Действующие

### <Цель — коротко, как он её сказал>
- **Зачем:** <his reason, in his words; a link to the idea or portrait line if there is one>
- **Срок:** <date, or «не назван»>
- **Признак:** <what shows progress — a number or an observable fact; «не назван» until he gives one>
- **Откуда:** <«сказал сам ГГГГ-ММ-ДД» / [[<идея>]] / [[<итог>]]>
- **Ход:** <one line per monthly review: [[2026-09]] — moved / stalled / why>

## Достигнутые и снятые

- <цель> — <date>, <reached / dropped and why in one line>
```

## Rules

- **Few goals.** When a sixth active goal is proposed, say that five are already active and ask
  which one gives way or whether he keeps six — his call, but name the cost: attention is his
  scarcest resource.
- **His words.** A goal, its reason and its deadline are his; never sharpen them into something he
  did not say. A missing deadline or measure stays «не назван», and the next review may ask for it.
- **Ход is written only by the monthly review**, one line per goal, with a link to that month's review.
- A reached or dropped goal moves to «Достигнутые и снятые» with one line — never deleted.
