# Reading a play session

"Works" is not "fun". A mechanic can follow its rules faithfully and still be boring; fun is
found by playing and asking every time "весело?".

## Before the owner plays

- Name the hypothesis in one phrase: "если игрок делает X, он чувствует Y — видно по Z". Z must
  be noticeable in play: restarts on his own, tries a trick without a hint, says "ещё".
- Give 3–5 concrete things to try, each testing a piece of the hypothesis; the last is "просто
  поиграй пару минут как хочешь". Explain only the controls, not the mechanic: if he does not
  understand what to do, that is a finding about readability, not a reason to explain.
- The main action already answers with a chord: a sound, a flash or particles, one camera
  reaction, a scale punch on the acting object — within the same 2–3 frames. Without response a
  good rule is judged boring. A placeholder sound is fine.

## The verdict

Ask first «весело?» with four answers, without recommending one: «да, хочу ещё», «нормально»,
«скучно», «раздражает». Then ask which concrete problems he felt — list ones specific to this
mechanic, not generic words. If he already described it in his own words, ask only what is
missing.

| verdict | what it means | what to change |
|---|---|---|
| да, хочу ещё | the hypothesis holds | small tuning, then the mechanic is accepted |
| нормально | works but does not grip | one more round of finding the fun; an idea pass for twists |
| скучно | no reason to try again | a rule, not numbers: where are risk, choice, reward for mastery, pace? |
| раздражает | controls or fairness get in the way | numbers and response: delay, readability, forgiveness |

"Скучно" is almost never cured by numbers.

## Finding the fun

| ingredient | present when | if missing, turn |
|---|---|---|
| meaningful choice | a decision every 5–15 s with a visible consequence | a fork (route, risk, resource); remove the obviously right option |
| risk and reward | the risky option pays noticeably better | widen the gap; make the risk readable |
| mastery | after 10 minutes he does the same thing better and notices | a skill ceiling (timing, combo, line) |
| surprise | something new in the last 2–3 minutes | variations, a rare event, two systems colliding |
| self-expression | two players play differently | builds, styles, own base |
| response | the action answers within 2–3 frames | the chord above |
| pacing | tension rises and falls, there is a peak | waves, breathers, a peak at the end |

When he says "скучно": ask for the best moment and amplify it; turn one knob at a time to the
extremes (×0.5, ×2) — fun often lives at the edge; add pressure (time, threat, scarcity);
remove steps between wanting and doing.

## When to stop

- One group of parameters per round, "было → стало" in numbers, otherwise nobody knows what
  worked. When he cannot describe a difference, give two variants to switch between and compare.
- Three tuning rounds without «да, хочу ещё» — stop turning numbers. Rephrase the hypothesis,
  change a rule or the control model, or ask him: переделать, упростить или вырезать.
- Cut a mechanic when two of these agree: unclear after two sessions; not one fun moment (a smile,
  "ещё раз", a voluntary restart); works only with explanations; three reworks. Cutting is his
  decision. Mark it "вырезана" in the mechanics table with the reason in one line, so nobody walks
  into the same dead end.
- "Нормально" accepted anyway is accepted with a note to return to it in polish. "Скучно" and
  "раздражает" are never accepted.

## What screenshots cannot tell

Weight, timing and sound sync are not visible on still frames. Never claim "feels good" from
screenshots — that is his to judge. Measure what can be measured (frames from input to
reaction) and leave the feel to him.
