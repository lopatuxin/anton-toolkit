# Idea pass — invent, the owner decides

A game is remembered for its hook: a mechanic the neighbours lack, a synergy of two systems, a
moment players retell. The interview captures what the owner wants; this pass adds what he
would not have thought of but will answer "да, именно".

## When

| moment | what to invent | how many to show |
|---|---|---|
| concept just approved | signature hooks: how the game differs from its reference | up to 4 |
| a core or signature mechanic about to be specified | 2–3 designs of it, one bold | 2–3 |
| a play verdict of "нормально" or "скучно" | twists that add choice, risk or feedback | 2–3 |
| the first playable piece feels flat | synergies of accepted mechanics, 1–2 mechanics that deepen the loop | up to 4 |
| before a batch of levels or content | the signature moment of each unit | one list |
| polishing | moments of delight: surprises, secrets, rare events | up to 4 |
| on request | what was asked | up to 4 |

Outside these moments do not generate ideas in advance. One pass per moment.

## Techniques — take 3–4 per pass, not all

- **Verb with a second meaning.** The player's main verb gains a second effect: drifting charges
  nitro, hiding reloads the weapon.
- **"Like X, but also Y".** Cross the reference with a distant genre: racing × rhythm (nitro on
  the beat), shooter × billiards (ricochets).
- **Invert a convention.** The monster fears the player's light; the one falling behind gets the
  best line.
- **Constraint as a hook.** Take something familiar away: no brakes, one bullet, no UI.
- **Risky twin.** Every safe action gets a riskier version with a bigger reward: a parry instead
  of a block.
- **Synergy matrix.** A mechanic × mechanic table; each cell is what happens when they meet. Two
  strong cells are already a hook.
- **The theme dictates a rule.** A subway game: the train is a timer and cover. A game about
  dreams: physics changes when the hero falls asleep.
- **One rule on top.** A rule added to a working verb that changes decisions: a jump leaves a
  platform for two seconds.
- **Emergence.** Systems that interact without a script: fire + wind, noise + enemy hearing.
- **Player expression.** Where two players play differently: build, style, base, a replay.
- **Backwards from the feeling.** Pick the feeling ("паника под контролем") and derive the
  mechanic that produces it.
- **A moment to retell.** Imagine what the player tells a friend, then the mechanic that makes it
  possible without a script.

## From ten to three

1. **Diverge**: 8–12 raw ideas. For a fresh view you may hand generation to a subagent that only
   reads the concept, mechanics and play notes and returns cards; it changes nothing.
2. **Converge** to 3–4 that pass every filter: violates no anti-pillar and serves a pillar; its
   fun fits one phrase; can be tried within one mechanic's work; its cost matches the game's
   scale; not rejected before (the ideas table).
3. Keep different kinds, not four variations of one.

## Idea card — five to seven lines

```
Нитро в такт
Нитро бьёт сильнее, если нажать в такт музыке.
Почему весело: гонка становится выступлением, мастерство видно и слышно.
Как проверить: кольцевая трасса с метрономом, три круга в такт и без.
Цена: средняя (синхронизация со звуком, индикатор такта). Риск: мешает тем, кто не слышит ритм.
Принцип: «скорость на грани».
```

Show the cards as text, ask which go in (several allowed, "ни одна" allowed). Every shown idea
becomes a row of the ideas table: proposed → accepted (becomes a mechanic with status "идея";
first version or later is the owner's call, later by default), rejected (reason in one phrase),
or deferred. An accepted idea is then built and played like any mechanic; if it is not fun in
play, it is cut like any other.

## Anti-patterns

- "Добавим крафт / прокачку / открытый мир" — a genre feature, not an idea. An idea changes the
  player's decisions in the 30-second loop.
- Twelve ideas to the owner — choosing becomes work. Three or four.
- "Сделать интереснее" — not checkable. Write the card.
- Building first, asking later — the decision is the owner's.
